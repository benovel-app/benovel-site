Privacy

Privacy Policy

BeNovel respects user privacy. This site only collects email, feedback, bug reports, and related system info when a user voluntarily fills out a form.

How data is used

  • Notify you about major releases.
  • Fix software issues.
  • Improve product features and experience.
  • Contact users willing to be interviewed.

Manuscript content

BeNovel stores your work locally by default. Unless you actively provide it via a form or email, this site does not collect your novel content.

Third-party services

The site may use GitHub Releases for downloads and Google Forms or Tally to collect email and feedback. When using external forms, data handling is also governed by that service's policy.

Data deletion

We do not sell your email or feedback. If you want your submitted email or feedback removed, contact benovel.official@gmail.com.
