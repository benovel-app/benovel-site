2026 / 07 / 12 App

BeNovel launches on July 19

The BeNovel app officially launches on Sunday, July 19, 2026 (UTC). Stay tuned.

From a local bookshelf and structured chapters to character and worldbuilding notes, reading mode, and manuscript export, the complete novel-writing workspace is almost ready for you. Your work stays on your device by default, so core writing remains available offline.

Thank you to every writer who followed the journey, tested early versions, and shared feedback. We'll see you in BeNovel on July 19.
