Chapter 1

Local library

Your whole story collection, on your machine

Pick up an existing draft, import a workspace package, or open a blank slot for something new. Covers, authors, genres, status, and character counts stay visible in one searchable local library.

  • Manage multiple works in one library
  • Search and filter by status or genre
  • Open, import, or create without leaving the shelf

Chapter 2

Draft & context

Write with the story world in view

Keep the manuscript beside a character profile, world note, or plot outline. The reference you need stays inside the same work, so checking a voice or detail never breaks the writing rhythm.

  • Chapters, characters, worldbuilding, and notes
  • Open prose and reference material side by side
  • One workspace instead of scattered files

Chapter 3

AI suggestions

Get another draft — not the final say

Select a passage, describe what you want to improve, and review the proposal directly in the editor. Every deletion and addition stays visible, and nothing replaces the manuscript until you accept it.

  • You choose the passage and the instruction
  • Proposed changes appear as a readable diff
  • Accept or discard before the manuscript changes
Optional by designThe library, editor, story notes, reading, comments, and export all work without configuring AI.

Chapter 4

Book view

Read it like a reader before you publish

Step out of the editor and into a calm, book-like spread. Chapter openings, paragraphs, spacing, and page turns make it easier to feel the pace of the story instead of staring at the tools used to write it.

  • Book-like two-page reading
  • Contents, chapter, and page navigation
  • A quieter view for judging pace and flow

Chapter 5

Comments

Review notes that stay attached to the words

Highlight a passage and leave a comment without rewriting it on the spot. Open, resolved, and archived notes turn revision into a clear list of decisions instead of a pile of reminders elsewhere.

  • Comments anchored to selected passages
  • Open, resolved, and archived states
  • Review the draft without leaving the workspace

Chapter 6

Connected works

One novel can grow into a whole story world

Create a sequel, prequel, interlude, after story, spin-off, or shared-world work from the manuscript already in front of you. Carry over the characters and context you need instead of rebuilding the world from scratch.

  • Six relationships between works
  • Carry over main characters and story context
  • Made for series, spin-offs, and shared worlds

Chapter 7

Export & packages

Export without locking your story in

Export the full manuscript, a chapter, or a section to Markdown, TXT, or DOCX. Tune fonts, spacing, paper, page numbers, and contents, or carry the complete workspace in a portable package.

  • Whole manuscript / chapter / section
  • Markdown · TXT · DOCX
  • Layout controls and portable workspace packages
