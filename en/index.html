Story workflow

Open a project like a book. Work on it like a studio.

BeNovel keeps the messy parts of long-form writing in one place, from the shelf to revision, from worldbuilding to final export.

01

Three-board workspace

Navigate chapters and notes on the left, write in the center, and keep AI context on the right.

02

Chapter structure

Track sections, draft status, summaries, notes, and chapter-level context without leaving the story.

03

Lore and character notes

Manage characters, worldbuilding, and style notes as part of the project, not as scattered side files.

04

AI revision flow

Ask for rewrites, polish, summaries, and consistency checks while keeping every change reviewable.

05

Chapter mode

Let AI propose a chapter plan first, then approve or reject changes item by item.

06

Reading and comments

Review pacing in a vertical reading mode and leave comments on your own draft, fully offline.

07

Export and packages

Export a section, chapter, or whole novel to Markdown, TXT, DOCX, or a project package.

08

Optional cloud sync

Connect GitHub for cross-device backup while your local files stay at the core.

Page II

Chapter I · Writing board

Open the first chapter and start writing.

The app is designed for the way novelists actually work: jumping between the sentence, the chapter, the cast, and the world.

Chapter II · Revision

Let AI mark the margin before it touches the manuscript.

BeNovel keeps AI helpful but bounded: proposals are visible, comparable, and easy to reject.

Chapter III · Structure

Read the AI's revision plan before restructuring a chapter.

Chapter mode handles larger structural edits: reorganizing chapters, adjusting sections, filling in notes, and updating character or lore data.

Chapter IV · Reading mode

Read the draft like a finished book before anyone else does.

Switch from editing to book-like vertical reading to catch rhythm, spacing, and pacing problems.
