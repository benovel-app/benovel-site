About us

Create with focus Finish every story

BeNovel is published by SparkAct. We believe a creative tool should never replace its creator. It should take care of the work around inspiration, helping every story stay organized, keep growing, and reach its final page.

About BeNovel

A long-form writing studio built for creators

BeNovel is a long-form writing tool designed around the creator.

From the project library, chapter structure, characters, and worldbuilding to reading, comments, backups, and manuscript export, BeNovel keeps the work of a long story in one place and reduces the friction of moving between scattered files and tools.

Your work and your creative pace remain yours. You decide where the story goes; BeNovel makes the path clearer, smoother, and easier to keep following.

About SparkAct

Turning ideas into products people can use

SparkAct is a startup focused on software development, digital products, and AI applications.

We bring together technology, design, and product thinking to understand real needs, improve workflows, and turn ideas into practical, extensible, and inventive digital products. From digital transformation to custom product development, we make technology more than a concept: a tool that solves problems, supports people, and creates lasting value.

BeNovel puts that belief into practice—starting from the real needs of creators, using technology to organize the creative process, and leaving the direction of every story in its creator's hands.
